Hiring mindset

Friendly, dependable, and ready for a busy café.

Coffee Cove is a small local shop where customer service, quality, and teamwork matter every day.

What we care about

A welcoming shop starts with the team.

Coffee Cove serves locals, families, and travelers heading toward the coast, PCC, and the North Shore. The right team members help every guest feel noticed, welcomed, and taken care of.

Experience can help, but attitude, dependability, and willingness to learn matter just as much.

Team values

What makes someone a good fit.

Coffee Cove is a busy, friendly, hands-on café environment. These are the qualities that help the shop run well.

🤙

Customer-service focused

You care about helping people feel welcome, respected, and taken care of every visit.

⏰

Dependable

🙌

Team-first

☕

Comfortable with morning rushes

The work

Help create the stop people are happy they found.

A great Coffee Cove shift means greeting guests, keeping drinks and food moving, helping the team, and making sure the shop feels clean, warm, and welcoming.

Some guests are on their way to work. Some are visiting the island. Some are grabbing coffee before a beach stop or North Shore drive. The goal is the same: make their stop feel easy and worth remembering.
